# SEO Audit — PlantAtHome storefront

Audited 2026-10-04 against code and live production (www.plantathome.in). Stack: Next.js 16 App Router; pages server-render (ISR/SSG); data from the Laravel API; sitemap and robots generated by `app/sitemap.ts` and `app/robots.ts`.

Status key: **Fixed** = shipped in the two SEO passes (sitelinks pass `0673477` + plant-delivery pass). **Open** = needs work or a business decision.

## Fixed

| # | Problem | Severity | Fix | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category pages were empty to crawlers: H1 "Loading…", no product links in the HTML | Critical | Server-prefetch the category and first product page with the client's exact query keys | `app/c/[slug]/page.tsx`, `src/framework/rest/ssr/prefetch.ts` |
| 2 | Staging was an indexable copy of production | Critical | `IS_INDEXABLE_SITE`: non-production hosts get `Disallow: /`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, an empty sitemap | `src/lib/site-url.ts`, `app/robots.ts`, `next.config.ts` |
| 3 | `plantathome.in` and `www.plantathome.in` both served pages | High | 308 apex → www | `next.config.ts` |
| 4 | `/plants` duplicated the homepage | High | `/plants` is a real Plants listing ("Buy Plants Online") | `app/(home)/[searchType]/page.tsx`, `src/components/layouts/plantathome.tsx` |
| 5 | Empty categories and coming-soon verticals in the sitemap | High | Empty categories noindexed and left out of the sitemap | `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/c/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| 6 | Internal links pointed at robots-blocked `/{type}/search` | High | Links go to `/{type}` and `/c/{slug}` | 14 components |
| 7 | `/policies/*` 404'd (Next 16 `params` is a Promise) | Medium | `await params` | `app/policies/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| 8 | Homepage title and H1 didn't target plant delivery | High | Title "PlantAtHome – Plant Delivery Online in India \| Buy Plants Online"; H1 "Plant Delivery Across India" (brand line kept as display text) | `app/(home)/page.tsx`, `src/components/storefront/home/hero-plant.tsx` |
| 9 | No plant-delivery landing page; the `/plants-in` hub competed for the same query | High | New `/plant-delivery` (how it works, categories, real products, cities, care); `/plants-in` 308s to it | `app/plant-delivery/page.tsx`, `src/page-bodies/plant-delivery.tsx` |
| 10 | City pages indexable regardless of real stock | High | `isCityIndexable`: admin flag AND ≥ 8 products in the product list's own `total` for that city; the page meta and the sitemap share it | `src/framework/rest/ssr/location-pages.ts`, `app/plants-in/[city]/page.tsx`, `app/sitemap.ts` |
| 12 | Product titles were the bare name | Medium | Plants: "Buy {name} Plant Online" when it fits in 60 chars; admin `seo_title` wins | `src/lib/seo.ts`, `app/products/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| 13 | WebSite SearchAction targeted a robots-blocked URL | Low | Removed (Google retired the sitelinks search box) | `app/(home)/page.tsx` |
| 14 | Vertical pages had no breadcrumb data | Low | BreadcrumbList JSON-LD | `app/(home)/[searchType]/page.tsx` |
| 15 | Variable products advertised one price | Medium | AggregateOffer (low/high/offerCount) | `app/products/[slug]/page.tsx` |

## Open

| # | Problem | Severity | Recommendation | Owner action? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Production has zero city landing pages** — `plantathome:seed-location-pages` was never run there, so every `/plants-in/{city}` 404s | Critical for city keywords | Run `plantathome:seed-location-pages --activate` on production (creates pages only for cities with live supply) | Yes — production data write |
| B | `/tools`, `/farm-box`, `/c/planters-pots` have no products in production | Medium | Stock them, or noindex until stocked | Yes — catalogue |
| C | Unverified claims in the hero: "500+ cities", "12k+ customers", "4.9/5 from 12,000+ reviews" | Medium (trust / structured-data policy) | Replace with real numbers or remove | Yes — real figures needed |
| D | No published legal policies in production (`/legal/public/policies` is empty) | Low | Publish them in admin; `/privacy` and `/terms` already work | Yes |
| E | Product reviews: AggregateRating only where real reviews exist; no Review objects | — | Correct as is; grows with real reviews | — |
| F | Organization has no phone or address | Low | Add only when a public support number and registered address are confirmed | Yes |
| G | `/plant-care` and `/plant-gifting` don't exist | Low | Build only with real content (care guides, gifting catalogue); thin pages hurt more than help | Yes — content |
| H | Cold `next build` can time out on `/sitemap.xml` (hundreds of sequential API calls) | Medium (build reliability) | Parallelise or paginate the sitemap fetches | No |

## What was checked and is fine

Titles and descriptions exist on every public route; canonicals point to `https://www.plantathome.in`; one H1 per page; product, category and city pages render real content in the initial HTML; alt text is set on product and category images (decorative images use `alt=""`); search and account pages are robots-blocked or noindex; city alias slugs (gurgaon → gurugram) 308 to one URL; Product, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage (cities), Organization and WebSite JSON-LD use only real data.
